Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

rclone-personal is a personal OAuth configuration used to connect rclone to the owner's Google Drive account.

Data Access

The application requests Google Drive permissions to read, create, modify, and delete files as instructed by its owner.

Data Storage

Authentication credentials and file data may be stored locally on the owner's device as part of the rclone configuration and file management process.

Data Sharing

The owner does not intentionally share Google Drive data with third parties through this OAuth configuration.

Contact

For inquiries, contact the developer using the support email listed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
